- **Diagnosis:** bilateral papilledema (raised intracranial pressure).

- **Findings:** pH 7.32; PaO2 12.4 kPa (≈ 93 mmHg) on FiO2 0.4 (P/F ≈ 233); PaCO2 5.9 kPa (≈ 44 mmHg); HCO3 28 mmol/L (28 mEq/L); lactate 1.2 mmol/L (≈ 11 mg/dL); BE +4.9; K 4.6 mmol/L (4.6 mEq/L).

@ Neuro_044_0 | Fundoscopy — 30M, right eye decreased vision (case 10)
- **Findings:** both optic discs look normal (no swelling, no pallor); exam sheet: right RAPD, reduced acuity, red desaturation, central scotoma.
- **Diagnosis:** retrobulbar optic neuritis ("the patient sees nothing and the doctor sees nothing").

@ Neuro_066_1 | Swinging flashlight test — 32F, right decreased vision (case 10)
- **Findings:** both pupils constrict when the light is on the normal eye; when the light swings to the affected eye, both pupils dilate.
- **Diagnosis:** relative afferent pupillary defect (Marcus Gunn pupil) → optic nerve lesion on the affected side.

@ Neuro_045_0 | MRI brain, FLAIR — same presentation (case 10)
- **Findings:** multiple ovoid hyperintense white-matter lesions — periventricular, perpendicular to the ventricles on sagittal views (Dawson fingers), callosal and juxtacortical; CSF oligoclonal bands positive (not in serum).
